The same spring model can become a suspension, an interface transition, a camera follow system or an impact reaction.

The equation is shared. The job changes.

A suspension follows a road input. An interface may be interrupted before its motion finishes. A camera must respond when its target reverses direction. An impact reaction has to recover after repeated impulses.

That’s the idea behind my Spring Physics Lab: several practical applications, useful tunables and comparisons you can inspect rather than just watch.

Changing damping can reduce oscillation, but the best setting depends on what the system needs to do. The lesson is in that trade-off.

Try the studies: https://pritishchoudhary.com/engineeringlab

These are controlled teaching models. Production vehicle, camera and UI systems still need their own constraints and validation.
